Our history

From one Calgary store to a Western Canadian network

In 1979, four friends (Mel Soloway, Jerry Miller, Ron Hall, and Moe Gagnon) opened the first Calgary Fasteners & Tools location.

Over the years, the company grew, adding locations in Burnaby, Surrey, Kelowna, Vernon, Penticton, Kamloops, Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge, Saskatoon, Winnipeg, and Brandon.

In 2024, The Fastener Group partnered with TriWest Capital Partners to bring our separate companies together under one unified organization. The following year, we expanded further with the acquisitions of JD Industrial Supplies in Regina and Ono Work and Safety in British Columbia.

As we continue to grow and integrate, we’re building a foundation that will make the next 50 years even stronger than the last.
