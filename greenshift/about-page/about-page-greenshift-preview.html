Our history

From one Calgary store to a Western Canadian network

  1. 1979

    Four friends, Mel Soloway, Jerry Miller, Ron Hall and Moe Gagnon, open the first Calgary Fasteners & Tools location.

  2. Growth

    Locations follow in Burnaby, Surrey, Kelowna, Vernon, Penticton, Kamloops, Edmonton, Red Deer, Lethbridge, Saskatoon, Winnipeg and Brandon.

  3. 2024

    The Fastener Group partners with TriWest Capital Partners, bringing our separate companies together as one organization.

  4. 2025

    We welcome JD Industrial Supplies in Regina and Ono Work and Safety in British Columbia.

As we continue to grow and integrate, we’re building a foundation that will make the next 50 years even stronger than the last.
