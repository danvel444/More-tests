Gift certificate policy statement

Gift Certificates

01

TFG Gift Certificates may be used toward the purchase of products at The Fastener Group branch locations.
02

TFG Gift Certificates are issued electronically. Each Gift Certificate is assigned a unique alphanumeric code and is delivered to the recipient by email.
03

The unique Gift Certificate code must be provided and entered exactly as issued when the Gift Certificate is redeemed.
04

Partial redemptions are permitted. Any unused balance remains available on the original Gift Certificate code for future purchases until the balance reaches zero.
05

If the Gift Certificate does not cover the full purchase amount, the customer must pay the remaining balance using an approved payment method.
06

Gift Certificates are not redeemable for cash except where required by applicable law.
07

Gift Certificates do not expire and are not subject to service, activation, or inactivity fees.
08

Lost, deleted, misdirected, altered, stolen, or unauthorized Gift Certificates will not be replaced or reissued. TFG may resend the original Gift Certificate email and code to the verified recipient; this does not constitute issuance of a replacement Gift Certificate.
09

Gift Certificates cannot be used to purchase additional Gift Certificates.
10

Refunds for purchases made using a Gift Certificate may be issued as a store credit or Gift Certificate at the discretion of TFG.
11

Gift Certificates are non-refundable and may not be returned.
12

TFG reserves the right to refuse, cancel, or hold Gift Certificates obtained through fraud, unauthorized resale, or other improper means.
13

Use of a Gift Certificate constitutes acceptance of these Terms and Conditions, which may be amended from time to time.
